How scoring works

100 XP for a drawing done on its day. 60 XP for catching up a missed day later. Challenge dice add +25 easy, +50 medium, +75 hard. Roll up to two a day; each die can be rerolled once. Streak bonus: +10% per on-time day in a row before today, up to +50%. Catch-ups keep your XP flowing but don't repair a broken streak. Day 31 done on Hallowe'en earns an extra +50.
